How to play

  • ⇆Move the farmer with WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere to steer.
  • ●Rats drop gold when your crossbows hit them. Walk close to coins to scoop them up.
  • ◆Rats come in many kinds, and each needs a different plan: fast rats race ahead, armored rats shrug off arrows, shield rats block arrows until their shield breaks (cannons fly over it), healer rats mend the crowd (take them out first), brood rats burst into ratlings when they fall, bomb rats blow up (pop them inside the crowd), ninja rats flip out of the way of arrows, and jetpack rats rocket forward. A card explains each new kind when it first appears, and the Rat guide on the title screen and pause menu has them all.
  • ▣Stand on a pad to pour coins into it. When it fills, a crossbow is built. Step off and back on to buy again.
  • ▲Every crossbow upgrades from level 1 to 7 on the pad beside it and gains a new ability each time: Power Shot, Twin Shot, Armor Piercer, Triple Barrage, Frost Arrows and Crystal Storm (see the Armory). After wave 2 and wave 5 you can also upgrade the town hall for more health, and the houses grow with it.
  • »Skip wave sends the next wave right away for bonus gold. Mid-wave it unlocks once every rat of the current wave is out. Turn on Auto to start each wave 3 seconds after the last one is cleared, and tap ▶ 1× (or press F) to play at double speed.
  • 2Two maps: Level 1, Sunstone Canyon, where the road winds round two red-rock plateaus, and Level 2, Lonetree Hill, where it zig-zags up and over a big sand hill past a lone flowering tree and a picnic. Pick one on the title screen.
  • ✚When rats damage the town hall, a repair pad appears in front of it. Stand on it to pour in gold and patch the hall back to full health: 1 gold for each point of health it lost.
  • ⌂The rats march on your town: a town hall, houses, two farms and the villagers who live there. Villagers stop and stare when rats come near, run home and hide if they get too close, and come back out once it is safe. Win and the whole town celebrates.
  • !Giant rats carry a lot of health and hit the gate hard. Every wave is harder than the last, and six bosses climb out of the burrow along the way. A siren and a red warning give you 5 seconds to get ready, and all of them return in the final wave. Survive all 16 waves to hold the canyon.
  • ☠Three bosses have tricks. The Sewer Slime splits into smaller slimes when it bursts. The Tunnel Mole digs under the canyon and comes up near the town (red cracks show where). The Canyon Buzzard flies over the road dropping rats, out of reach of cannon shells, so crossbows must shoot it down.

Every wave you clear earns diamonds, even if the town hall falls later: on Normal 1 for each of waves 1–5, 2 for waves 6–10, 3 for waves 11–15 and 20 for the final wave, so 50 for a full clear. Easy pays 30 for a full clear and Hard pays double, 100. Spend 100 in the Armory to unlock the Cannon Tower, then pick it with the cannon button (or press 2) before stepping on a build pad. At level 4 each cannon takes one of two paths: the fast Rapid Launcher or the mighty Thunder Mortar.

Every wave you clear earns diamonds: on Normal 1 each for waves 1–5, 2 each for waves 6–10, 3 each for waves 11–15 and 20 for the final wave. A full clear of all 16 waves earns 30 on Easy, 50 on Normal and 100 on Hard.

Ride into battle! The farmer rides the animal you pick in every game and runs much faster, so you reach coins and pads sooner.

Paint every crossbow and cannon, their arrows and the farmer's pitchfork. Skins only change the look.

Crossbow Tower

Fast, precise attacks. Abilities stack as it levels up.

Owned
  1. 1Single Shot: fires one arrow at one enemy.
  2. 2Power Shot: fires faster and deals more damage.
  3. 3Twin Shot: two arrows together, hitting one enemy twice or two separate enemies.
  4. 4Armor Piercer: heavy arrows ignore most of a giant rat's armour and pass through up to two enemies.
  5. 5Triple Barrage: three crossbows fire together. Every fifth attack launches a rapid burst.
  6. 6Frost Arrows: crystal arrows slow enemies. Three hits on the same enemy briefly freeze it. Bosses only slow down.
  7. 7Crystal Storm: purple arrows bounce between nearby enemies, and every few seconds an arrow storm rains down.

Cannon Tower

Slower shots with explosive area damage.

Unlocked
  1. 1Cannonball: fires an explosive ball that damages enemies close to the impact.
  2. 2Heavy Blast: bigger explosions deal more damage and push small rats back.
  3. 3Double Cannon: two cannonballs in quick succession, at the same group or two groups.

At level 4, choose one of two paths. Each cannon takes its own path, so you can build both.

Rapid Launcher

Fast, light shells that shred swarms and fast rats. Weak against armour.

  1. 4Rapid Launcher: four tubes fire a light shell every third of a second, each at a fresh group.
  2. 5Cluster Shells: every shell bursts into three bomblets that scatter over the crowd.
  3. 6Firestorm: fires even faster, and every shell sets the ground on fire.
  4. 7Rocket Barrage: a rocket pod. Every 4 seconds a salvo of 14 rockets hits the front of the crowd.

Thunder Mortar

Slow, enormous blasts that crush crowds, giants and bosses. Fast rats can slip past.

  1. 4Thunder Mortar: one enormous shell every few seconds. Double damage to armour; it stuns and throws rats back.
  2. 5Triple Thunder: all three barrels fire together, flattening a long stretch of the road.
  3. 6Crystal Quake: twin crystal shells send out a slowing shockwave and crack armour for 4 seconds.
  4. 7Doomsday Nova: a huge armour-breaking Nova every 2 seconds, with side cannons firing in between.
