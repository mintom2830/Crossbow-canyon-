Sky String

Tap at the right moment and a blast of wind hits your kite, sending it flying sideways across the fields. The farther it flies, the more money you earn. When the rope runs out, it stretches tight and swings the kite around. If the rope holds, the kite swings down and lands. If it snaps, the kite flies away.

How to play: a power bar slides back and forth. Tap, click, or press Space to send the wind. Stop the bar in the gold ★ zone for a Perfect blast. Upgrade the wind to hit harder, the kite to glide farther, and the rope to go longer and stay strong. Rope that runs out stretches tight and swings the kite round. A safe landing pays a 25% bonus. If the rope snaps, the loose kite keeps flying on its own until it lands, but every metre after the snap pays half.
