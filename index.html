Sky String

Tap at the right moment and a blast of wind hits your kite, sending it flying sideways across the fields. The farther it flies, the more money you earn. When the rope runs out, the kite jerks to a stop. If the rope holds, the kite swings down and lands. If it snaps, the kite flies away.
